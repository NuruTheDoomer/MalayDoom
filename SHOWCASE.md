# Showcase

This page will showcase parts of Doom that is translated into Malay, be it intermission screens, menus, text screens, etc.

This page only shows screenshots of MalayDoom in action, check [this playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOC6cxtHfiOQ) out for the showcase videos of MalayDoom in action!
