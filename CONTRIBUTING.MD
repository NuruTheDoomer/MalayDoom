# Contributing to MalayDoom

Thank you for your interest in contributing to MalayDoom!

MalayDoom is currently a mostly solo project, but contributions, testing, suggestions, and feedback from the Doom community are welcome.

## What You Can Contribute

There are many ways to help with MalayDoom, including:

- Translation review and corrections

- Suggestions for improving existing translations

- Localized graphics and other graphical resources

- Source port compatibility testing

- Testing different configurations

- Documentation improvements

- Source port compatibility information

- Bug reports

- Other improvements related to MalayDoom

You do not need to be a translator to contribute. Testing the WADs and reporting issues can be just as useful.

## Translation Contributions

MalayDoom aims to use natural Malaysian Malay while preserving the tone, humor, wordplay, terminology, and character of the original games.

When suggesting a translation change, please consider:

- Does the wording sound natural in Malaysian Malay?

- Is the original meaning and context preserved?

- Does the joke, wordplay, or unusual wording still work?

- Does the suggestion fit established MalayDoom terminology?

- Should a proper name remain untranslated?

Not every difference in wording needs to be changed. A translation does not necessarily need to match a particular person's preferred wording.

Suggestions are most useful when the existing translation is clearly incorrect, confusing, unnatural, or unsuitable for its context.

For larger translation changes, please discuss the proposed changes before making extensive modifications. This helps avoid duplicated work and keeps localization decisions consistent.

## Sumbangan Terjemahan

MalayDoom berusaha menggunakan Bahasa Melayu Malaysia yang semula jadi sambil mengekalkan nada, humor, permainan kata, istilah dan identiti asal permainan.

Apabila mencadangkan perubahan terjemahan, sila pertimbangkan perkara berikut:

- Adakah ayat tersebut kedengaran semula jadi dalam Bahasa Melayu Malaysia?

- Adakah maksud dan konteks asal dikekalkan?

- Adakah jenaka, permainan kata atau penggunaan bahasa yang unik masih berkesan?

- Adakah cadangan tersebut selaras dengan istilah yang telah digunakan dalam MalayDoom?

- Patutkah nama khas dikekalkan dalam bahasa asal?

Tidak semua perbezaan pilihan kata perlu diubah. Sesuatu terjemahan tidak semestinya perlu mengikut pilihan kata seseorang secara tepat.

Cadangan paling berguna ialah cadangan yang membetulkan terjemahan yang jelas salah, mengelirukan, janggal atau tidak sesuai dengan konteksnya.

Bagi perubahan terjemahan yang besar, sila bincangkan cadangan tersebut terlebih dahulu sebelum membuat perubahan secara meluas. Langkah ini membantu mengelakkan kerja yang bertindih dan memastikan keputusan terjemahan kekal konsisten.

## Source Port Compatibility Testing

Testing MalayDoom with different Doom source ports and versions is especially useful.

When reporting a compatibility issue, please include as much of the following as possible:

- Source port and version

- Game or expansion being used

- MalayDoom WAD being loaded

- Any additional PWADs, DeHackEd/BEX patches, or other addons

- Command line and load order, where relevant

- Description of the issue

- Screenshots, demo recordings, or other evidence, if useful

Compatibility information may be added to `COMPATIBILITY.md` once it has been sufficiently verified.

## Bug Reports

If you encounter a problem with MalayDoom, please provide enough information for the issue to be reproduced.

Useful bug reports should identify the affected WAD or resource, source port, version, and relevant configuration.

When possible, distinguish between:

- Translation errors

- Incorrect or missing graphics

- Source port compatibility issues

- Incorrect WAD load order

- Problems caused by third-party PWADs, DeHackEd/BEX patches, or other addons

This helps determine whether an issue belongs to MalayDoom itself or to another part of the setup.

If you are unsure whether something is a bug or an intended localization choice, feel free to ask or discuss it first.

## Credits and Attribution

Contributors whose work is included in MalayDoom will be credited in `CREDITS.md` and/or other appropriate documentation.

Please only submit material that you have the right to contribute.

If your contribution is based on or incorporates third-party material, please identify its source and any relevant licensing or permission requirements.

## Licensing and Permissions

Contributing to MalayDoom does not automatically transfer ownership of your original work to the project.

Contributors retain ownership of their original contributions unless they explicitly agree to different terms.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the necessary rights to provide it for inclusion in MalayDoom.

For information about the project's ownership and redistribution rules, see [PERMISSIONS.md](PERMISSIONS.md).

## Final Notes

MalayDoom is intended to be a community-oriented fan localization project rather than a replacement for the original Doom releases.

Contributions should aim to improve the localization while respecting the original games, their creators, and the rights of other contributors.

Most importantly, there is no single "correct" way to translate every line. MalayDoom's goal is to find wording that feels natural, fits the context, and still feels like Doom.
