# Permissions

## MalayDoom

MalayDoom is an unofficial fan-made localization project for Classic Doom and its official expansions.

Original translations, documentation, compatibility definitions, graphics, and other material created specifically for MalayDoom remain the property of their respective authors and contributors, unless stated otherwise.

Please do not redistribute, modify, or incorporate MalayDoom material into other projects without permission, unless the relevant material is explicitly released under terms that allow such use.

If you would like to use MalayDoom material in another project, please contact the maintainer first.

## Doom Game Assets

MalayDoom does not claim ownership of the original Doom game assets.

The original IWADs, graphics, music, sound effects, text, trademarks, and other game assets remain the property of their respective rights holders.

MalayDoom's localization WADs are intended to be used with legally obtained copies of the required Doom games and expansions. They are not replacements for the original IWADs.

MalayDoom does not distribute the required IWADs and official expansion PWADs. Users must obtain the required game files separately.

## MIDI Packs

Some MalayDoom releases provide compatibility addons for third-party MIDI replacement packs.

These addons contain compatibility definitions or related resources; they are **not the MIDI packs themselves**.

MalayDoom does not redistribute third-party MIDI packs. Users should obtain the packs separately and follow their respective licenses and redistribution requirements.

MalayDoom may include contributions from other members of the Doom community, including translation reviews, graphics, compatibility testing, documentation, and other project work.

Contributors retain ownership of their original contributions unless they explicitly agree to different terms.

By submitting material for inclusion in MalayDoom, contributors confirm that they have the necessary rights to provide it for that purpose.

Any specific licensing or redistribution terms supplied by a contributor should be documented where appropriate and respected when using or distributing the relevant material.

## Redistribution

Do not redistribute MalayDoom alongside copyrighted third-party material unless you have the necessary permission to redistribute that material.

When redistributing MalayDoom files, retain relevant credits, copyright notices, and permission information.

Some files or components may have additional licensing or redistribution requirements. Where applicable, those requirements take precedence over the general guidance in this document.
